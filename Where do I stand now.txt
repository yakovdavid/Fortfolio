### The Strategic Pivot: Moving to Institutional Grade
We are transitioning the app from a technical-only tracker with free-text allocations to an Institutional Grade system. Free-text allocations are an engineering risk because they allow philosophical contradictions (e.g., trying to run a passive-aggressive 80/0/20 portfolio using heavy infrastructure dividend stocks as the sole Quality driver). 

To fix this, we are locking the architecture into strict templates and introducing Fundamental Risk tracking alongside Technical Risk.

### Updated "Fortress" Investment Constitution
1. **Strict Templates, Not Percentages:** Users will select predefined strategic templates (e.g., Tactical Momentum 70/20/10, Hyper-Quality 80/0/20).
2. **Dynamic Quality Thresholds:** The required ROIC for the Quality layer changes based on the template:
   - *Tactical (70/20/10):* ROIC > 8% (Allows heavy infrastructure like KMI, CNI, because Satellites provide the Alpha).
   - *Hyper-Quality (80/0/20):* ROIC > 15% (Satellites are 0%, so the Quality layer must drive Alpha via Capital Light compounders like V, MA).
3. **Fundamental Alerts:** The app will actively monitor business performance, not just price action. If a stock's ROIC drops below the active template's threshold, it triggers a red `[FUNDAMENTAL DEGRADATION]` alert.
4. **Single Source of Truth:** We are abandoning basic APIs (like Yahoo Finance) for fundamental metrics, as their ROIC calculations (often Net Income based) are distorted. We will integrate Financial Modeling Prep (FMP) to pull accurate, NOPAT-based TTM data.
